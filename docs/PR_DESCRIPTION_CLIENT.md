# Marlow Dental — What We Built and How to Try It

A warm, plain-English walkthrough for the practice owner. No technical jargon.

## 1. What This Is

Welcome to the completed practice workspace for Marlow Dental. This platform runs your entire clinic from a single, unified system: your patient-facing website, online appointment booking, front-desk reception, clinical care operatories, and back-office practice management. Whether a patient is booking their first cleaning on a smartphone or a doctor is examining teeth in a treatment room, every part of the practice stays in continuous sync.

Every member of your team gets a tailored workspace designed specifically for their day-to-day responsibilities. Front-desk staff manage schedules, intake, patient arrivals, and payments without seeing private clinical examination records. Doctors have quick, distraction-free access to medical charts, dental diagrams, and treatment plans without having to handle billing ledgers or website copy. Practice owners enjoy complete visibility over daily revenue, cancellations, recalls, and staff activities.

Most importantly, nothing is shared across roles that should remain private. Your patient records stay secure, every staff action is recorded, and the system is loaded with realistic practice data and ready for you to explore right now.

## 3. A Day at the Clinic — Walkthrough

### Scene 1 — A patient books online
It is Sunday evening, and a new patient named Tariq feels mild sensitivity in an upper molar. He opens the Marlow Dental website on his phone, reads about preventive care and dental fillings, and taps "Book Appointment." He chooses Dr. Marcus Vance as his preferred dentist, selects a Tuesday morning visit, and submits his contact details along with a short note explaining his sensitivity. Within seconds, Tariq receives a clear on-screen confirmation reassuring him that the front desk will review his request first thing in the morning.

### Scene 2 — Sarah (receptionist) triages the request
On Monday morning at 8:00 AM, Sarah sits down at the front desk and opens her reception dashboard. Her "Needs Action" queue highlights Tariq's weekend booking request right at the top. She opens the request, reviews his preferred Tuesday morning time against Dr. Marcus's open chair schedule, and confirms the appointment with a single click. The clinic system instantly reserves the chair time, creates Tariq's new patient file, and prepares an automated reminder message scheduled to reach his phone before the visit.

### Scene 3 — The patient arrives and checks in
On Tuesday at 9:55 AM, Tariq walks into the practice and approaches the front desk. Sarah greets him by name, clicks "Check In," and records his arrival. Instantly, Tariq's name appears on the reception waiting lounge screen alongside an active elapsed-time counter showing that he has been waiting for two minutes. Sarah can see at a glance who has been waiting longest in the reception lounge, ensuring no patient sits unnoticed past their appointment time.

### Scene 4 — Dr. Marcus (doctor) opens the chart
Inside Operatory 2, Dr. Marcus looks at his chair-side screen, where his "Waiting for You" list shows that Tariq is checked in and ready. Dr. Marcus opens Tariq's chart and is immediately greeted by a prominent red medical alert banner highlighting Tariq's penicillin allergy. During the examination, Dr. Marcus taps upper right tooth #14 on the interactive dental chart to mark localized enamel decay. He types his structured clinical findings, documents his recommended tooth-colored filling, and signs the clinical note with his secure electronic signature to lock the record permanently.

### Scene 5 — The visit wraps up
Before Tariq leaves the chair, Dr. Marcus creates a straightforward two-phase treatment proposal outlining the recommended filling and a routine preventive cleaning for next month. With one click on "Finish Visit," the doctor completes the examination, which automatically creates a front-desk checkout task on Sarah's screen. Tariq walks up to the front desk, where Sarah opens his billing ledger, records his payment for today's diagnostic exam, and hands him a clean printed receipt clearly marked as an official payment record.

### Scene 6 — Sarah sends a recall reminder
Later that afternoon, Sarah checks the clinic's preventive recall list to see which established patients are due for their six-month cleanings. She reviews three patients whose preventive care windows are approaching and clicks to dispatch recall notices. The message log shows each dispatch clearly, displaying an honest "simulated" notice that shows clinic staff exactly what the reminder says while making it obvious that no live phone provider has been billed yet.

### Scene 7 — The practice owner checks the numbers
At the end of the day, Dr. Sarah Marlow, the clinic owner, logs in from her administrative dashboard to review the practice's overall performance. She opens the daily operations report to see total visits seen, cancellations, preventive recalls scheduled, and total revenue collected at the front desk. Curious about a canceled afternoon slot, she opens the clinic activity log, which shows a permanent, minute-by-minute record of who created, confirmed, or adjusted every appointment throughout the entire day.

## 4. What a Receptionist Will Love

- **All-in-one morning triage**: Incoming web bookings appear in a single prioritized queue, ready to accept or decline in seconds.
- **Visual waiting lounge timer**: An automatic elapsed-minute counter shows exactly how long each checked-in patient has been seated in the lobby.
- **Double-booking protection**: The appointment calendar automatically flags and prevents overlapping chair bookings while still allowing front-desk overrides when necessary.
- **Clear checkout tasks**: When a doctor clicks "Finish Visit" in the operatory, a checkout task appears on the front-desk screen with all instructions ready.
- **Instant payment recording**: Quickly take payments, track outstanding patient balances, and print clean patient receipts right from the desk.
- **Built-in follow-up reminders**: Track pending patient recalls, callback requests, and unconfirmed bookings without writing paper sticky notes.

## 5. What a Doctor Will Love

- **"Waiting for You" queue**: See which patients have arrived and how long they have been in the waiting area before calling them back.
- **High-visibility medical alerts**: Prominent allergy and medical condition banners stay visible at the top of the chart so safety is never missed.
- **Interactive dental chart**: Tap individual teeth on adult or pediatric diagrams to map conditions, restorations, crowns, or missing teeth in seconds.
- **One-click note templates**: Pull in standard examination templates to write comprehensive clinical notes quickly, then sign and lock them securely.
- **Multi-phase treatment proposals**: Group planned treatments into clear stages with transparent cost estimates that patients can easily understand.
- **Confidential clinical records**: Upload and view patient consent forms and diagnostic files directly within the patient's private chart.

## 6. What the Practice Owner Will Love

- **Executive business reports**: Instant visibility into patient numbers, cancellation rates, preventive recall retention, and daily revenue collections.
- **Permanent activity history**: A tamper-evident log records every booking created, appointment changed, note signed, and payment accepted.
- **Strict staff role privacy**: Front-desk staff cannot view private clinical notes, and doctors do not have to manage practice financial settings.
- **Customizable practice profile**: Update clinic operating hours, holiday closures, service price lists, and website content anytime without outside help.
- **Automated patient communication oversight**: Review all reminder notices and message histories with honest indicators of delivery status.
- **Continuity across devices**: Run front-desk intake, operatory charting, and owner reporting simultaneously with reliable clinic records.

## 9. What's Not Included Yet

- **Live text message and phone delivery**: Automated appointment reminders currently run in demonstration mode and are clearly marked as simulated until your practice connects an active business SMS or messaging phone line.
- **Third-party insurance claims processing**: This version tracks direct patient payments, balances, and receipts; direct electronic insurance portal claim filing is not included.
- **Physical paper receipt printing hardware**: Generating printable payment receipts is fully supported on-screen, but physical paper printouts require a receipt printer connected to the front-desk computer.
- **Direct digital radiography device integration**: You can upload and attach digital X-ray image files directly to patient charts, but direct hardware cable capture from sensor wands is not integrated in this release.
